Our Story

Welcome to Crostick, where craftsmanship meets personal expression. Founded in 2023, we specialize in creating one-of-a-kind crochet keychains that combine traditional techniques with modern personalization.

Each keychain is meticulously handcrafted by our team of artisans who pour their passion into every stitch. We believe that everyday items should spark joy and reflect your unique personality.
